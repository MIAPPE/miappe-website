Projects

MIAPPE trainings have aimed at providing biologists the knowledge necessary to curate their datasets, publish and share them. Computer scientists were given the information needed to enable MIAPPE in their systems and tools. Past training are listed below. Training material is freely available for reuse. You can access it by following the provided links.

  • 2023-06: FAIR data PLANT PHENO 2023. In French
  • 2023-06: Biohackathon Japan MIAPPE Introduction
  • 2023-05: Avignon data management training
  • 2021-09: Paris Moulon Fair data management for computer scientists and biologists
  • 2020-02: Paris Data Management. See full description on ELIXIR TESS
  • 2019: Nordplant training
  • 2018: Wheat initiative data management training and workshop (no material publicly available)
