Steering Commitee

Development of MIAPPE is overseen by a steering committee whose members represent interested countries, institutes and projects. Members of this committee can also be active developers of the standard and currently comprise:

  • Daniel Arend (Leibniz-Institut Für Pflanzengenetik und Kulturpflanzenforschung, Gatersleben, Germany)

Join the MIAPPE community

To be informed of new developments in MIAPPE, please consider subscribing to the MIAPPE mailing list. Traffic is low, but it subscribers are informed of future meetings, proposed revisions to the standard, and new tools.
